## securagen.ai

**Secure AI. Controlled action.**

securagen.ai P.C. is a European product company building products for cryptographic validation, information intelligence, regulatory and certification assurance, and sovereign AI. Alongside these, we develop verifiable controls for high-consequence actions by people and AI agents.

Our principle is simple: **evidence comes before trust**, whether the result is a cryptographic test, an intelligence assessment, an assurance finding or an AI answer.

### What we build

| Domain | Focus |
|---|---|
| **Cryptographic validation** | Classical, post-quantum and lightweight cryptography tested against reference implementations, including ML-KEM, ML-DSA, SLH-DSA and Ascon-AEAD128, with randomness testing and supply-chain analysis. |
| **Information intelligence** | OSINT collection, narrative analysis, fact-checking and source grading for traceable counter-FIMI reporting. |
| **Regulatory & certification assurance** | NIS2 readiness, CRA product conformity and Common Criteria / EUCC evaluation support. |
| **Sovereign document AI** | Offline and air-gapped document question-answering with local inference, source citations and evidence checks. |

Our automation supports expert decisions; it does not issue certification or legal conformity verdicts.
